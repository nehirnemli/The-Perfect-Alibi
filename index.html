01 — THE INCIDENT

A masterpiece.
A missing minute.

At an invitation-only preview in an exclusive Izmir gallery, a priceless painting vanished without a trace.

The Last Light, valued at approximately $8 million, was loaned to the gallery by a private family collection. The gallery had permission to exhibit the work — not to sell it.

At the end of the evening, the painting was gone. Security cameras had been offline for exactly ten minutes. Four people had access, opportunity, or a reason to lie.

Your task is to examine the evidence, compare statements, and decide who physically moved the painting.

But remember: being involved doesn't always mean being on the same side. Some people had plans. Others had secrets of their own.
